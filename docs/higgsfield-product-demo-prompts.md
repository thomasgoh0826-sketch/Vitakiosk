# Higgsfield Product Demo Prompts

These prompts wrap real product UI captures in cinematic context. Use real
VitaKiosk/ERP captures when safe. If real captures are unavailable, keep the
asset labelled as `Prototype`, `Internal Lab Build`, or `Placeholder`.

## VitaKiosk iPad Product Education

Create a cinematic 12-second product demo wrapper around a provided VitaKiosk
iPad screen capture. The device sits in a premium dark healthcare-tech lab with
cyan source-of-truth light paths. Show general product education, mock product
facts, shelf guidance, and pharmacist assistance. No diagnosis, no prescription
consultation, no real patient identity, no hospital logo. End text:
"General product education with staff escalation."

## VitaKiosk Large Kiosk Waiting Area

Create a 12-second premium kiosk presentation. A large VitaKiosk display stands
near a pharmacy or clinic waiting area using fictional signage only. The screen
shows mock-mode product education and QR direction. Staff remain visible as the
human escalation point. No medical claims. End text: "Guide before the counter."

## VitaFlow Source-of-Truth Wrap

Create a 12-second operations sequence using a safe VitaFlow ERP demo capture.
The dashboard appears as the source system behind product, stock, promotion,
purchase workflow, and branch data. Do not show customer names, sales records,
private branch data, or real database values. End text:
"ERP facts power every customer-facing surface."

## Partner Campaign Handoff

Create a 12-second partner campaign sequence. A reviewed sponsored product
education panel is clearly labelled. VitaKiosk shows participating pharmacy
partner info and QR redemption direction. The copy must not imply doctor
endorsement, hospital recommendation, or guaranteed results. End text:
"Reviewed education. Clear partner direction."
