# Site Compliance Wording

## Required Disclaimer

VitaKiosk provides general product education, where-to-buy guidance, and staff/pharmacist escalation. It does not provide diagnosis, prescription drug consultation, or professional medical advice.

## Sponsored Content Note

Sponsored product education must be clearly labelled and reviewed before display. Real deployment must follow local healthcare advertising and institutional compliance rules.

## Avoid

- AI doctor.
- AI diagnosis.
- Prescription drug consultation.
- Replaces pharmacist.
- Hospital recommends this product.
- Doctor endorses this pharmacy.
- Guaranteed treatment outcome.

## Prefer

- General product education.
- General supplement information.
- Pharmacy partner discovery.
- Where-to-buy guidance.
- Sponsored product education.
- Pharmacist/staff escalation available.
