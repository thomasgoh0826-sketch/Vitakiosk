# Site Compliance Wording

## Required Disclaimer

VitaKiosk provides general product education, where-to-buy guidance, and
staff/pharmacist escalation. It does not provide diagnosis, prescription drug
consultation, or professional medical advice.

## Sponsored Content Note

Sponsored product education must be clearly labelled and reviewed before
display. Real deployment must follow local healthcare advertising and
institutional compliance rules.

## Avoid

- AI doctor
- Diagnosis
- Prescription drug consultation
- Pharmacist replacement
- Hospital recommends product
- Doctor endorses product or pharmacy
- Guaranteed treatment result

## Use Instead

- General product education
- Where-to-buy guidance
- Participating pharmacy partners
- Reviewed sponsored product education
- Staff/pharmacist escalation
- Source-of-truth ERP data
- Missing data unavailable
