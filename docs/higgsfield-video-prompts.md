# Higgsfield Video Prompts

Use these prompts as reviewed storyboard inputs. Do not include real hospital
logos, real patient identity, doctor endorsements, diagnosis claims,
prescription consultation claims, or pharmacist replacement claims.

## Clinic Queue Problem

Create a cinematic 20-second healthcare-tech ad. A clinic front desk is busy
with patients waiting. One patient only wants general supplement/product
information but the queue is long. A premium AI kiosk beside the waiting area
lights up and shows general product education, QR direction, and staff
escalation. Professional healthcare-tech tone. No real hospital logo, no real
patient identity, no medical claims. End text: "Product education before the
counter."

## Pharmacy Partner Discovery

Create a 20-second premium healthcare retail video. A patient leaves a clinic
interested in a supplement/product but does not know where to buy it. VitaKiosk
shows participating pharmacy partner information, availability, QR direction,
and campaign redemption code. Do not imply doctor endorsement. End text:
"Connect product interest to participating pharmacy partners."

## Retail Pharmacy Promotion

Create a 20-second retail pharmacy tech ad. A pharmacy has product campaigns and
promotion leaflets, but staff are busy and customers do not hear the promotion
clearly. VitaKiosk displays sponsored product education and promotion leaflet in
a premium kiosk interface. Customer scans QR and walks to shelf. End text:
"Turn silent promotions into guided product discovery."

## AI Website Studio

Create a 20-second business transformation ad. A small business website looks
outdated and does not capture leads. The scene transforms into a premium
AI-ready website with service sections, chatbot, booking form, lead dashboard,
and launch animation. End text: "Websites that explain, capture, and convert."

## AI Academy

Create a 20-second modern education ad. A business owner and team use AI
randomly without workflow. Scene transitions into guided AI training: prompt
workflow, Codex build, automation board, content calendar, and website launch.
End text: "Learn AI as a practical business workflow."
